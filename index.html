01 / THE PROLOGUE

The world gives us matter.
We give it meaning.

Autarkeia begins with a simple question: what can we create when we pay closer attention? To the world around us. To the truth of our experience. To the person we are becoming.

Here, a physical gesture becomes a digital response. It is a small experiment in a larger idea: understanding reality is only the beginning. What we choose to do with that understanding is our responsibility.
