Why Teachable Machine?

It makes machine learning tangible: collect examples, train an image model and test it on something new. This project runs its five-class model in your browser.

Why does waste classification matter?

Separating materials helps you choose an appropriate collection or reuse route. Local collection rules, contamination and mixed materials still determine what can actually be recycled.

What do Gemini and OpenAI add?

A vision-capable assistant reviews the image alongside the local prediction and explains possible materials, handling and reuse. If the first service fails, the second can be tried. The result shows which service answered.

Does high confidence mean the answer is correct?

No. Confidence measures the model’s preference among its trained classes. It can be confident about an unfamiliar or incorrect object. New objects and independent test images are essential for evaluating accuracy.

Can a photo reveal the exact chemical composition?

Usually not. Packaging marks and material specifications provide stronger evidence. The assistant distinguishes visible clues from possible materials and asks for markings when identification is uncertain.

Where does my photo go?

The Teachable Machine prediction runs on your device. When you select “Analyse with AI” or send a follow-up, your selected image and message are sent to the responding AI service. This website does not save your images or conversation.
